Fundamental Concepts of using AI

AI reflects data, not facts

AI, at it's core is a LLM (large language model) that will answer questions according to the data it has been given. When the data that the AI has been trained on is full of biases and unreliable sources, then the AI won't be reliable either (Grok). So remember to fact check AI, instead of using AI to fact check people.

Evaluating AI

Teenagers need to question what they see and not what they read. Ai can generate images, videos, and audio in addition to text.
